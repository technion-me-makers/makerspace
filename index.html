Come build something.

This is the Technion Undergraduate Projects Workshop at the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering. Four 3D printers, a CNC router, an electronics bench and two long build tables, all in one room. Open 08:00–15:00. Scroll to fly through it.

Find a machine

Scroll to fly

  1. 01of 10

    Front door

    Come in through the double doors. The tall cabinet beside them is the hardware wall: bins of screws, sorted by length and labelled by head type, nuts and washers.

    • Cap-head and countersunk screws
    • M2 to M12, from 5 to 50 mm long

  2. 02of 10

    Electronics bench

    A bench the full width of the room, with sockets at every seat. Adjustable power supplies sit on the worktop; multimeters, scales and electronics kits are in the cabinets above.

    • Adjustable DC bench supplies
    • Digital multimeters
    • Compact digital scales
    • Kits of boards, jumper wires and parts
    Safety sheet and instructions

  3. 03of 10

    Soldering corner

    Hakko FX-888D stations share one fume extractor, with a flexible arm for each iron. Set the temperature, pull the arm over your work, and solder.

    • Hakko FX-888D soldering stations
    • Fume extractor with two arms
    • Cutting mats at each place
    Safety sheet and instructions

  4. 04of 10

    Build tables

    Two tables with eight seats each. Every place has a cutting mat, there are sockets at the table, and a screwdriver set lives at the wall end. The racks overhead carry the lighting and the tool cases.

    • 16 seats at two tables
    • Cutting mats at every place
    • Screwdriver and bit sets

  5. 05of 10

    Hand tools

    The Beta roller cabinet for each desk, goes wherever the work is. Every socket, wrench and driver has its own foam cut-out, so you can see at a glance what’s missing before you leave.

    • 1/2″ and 1/4″ ratchets, sockets up to 32 mm
    • Combination and offset ring wrenches
    • Screwdrivers, hex and Torx bits
    Safety sheet and instructions

  6. 06of 10

    Materials wall

    Filament for the printers is boxed by colour in the cabinets. Underneath is another work bench, with sockets along its whole length.

    • Filament, sorted by colour
    • Extra bench space
    • Compressed air on the pillar

  7. 07of 10

    3D printers

    Four Bambu Lab H2D printers, each with an AMS 2 Pro on top that feeds and swaps filament during a print. There’s a PC on the same bench and storage drawers underneath.

    • 4 × Bambu Lab H2D
    • AMS 2 Pro multi-material feeders
    • PC at the bench
    Safety sheet and instructions

  8. 08of 10

    CNC router

    A Tormach xsTECH Pro router inside a full enclosure, for the parts a printer can’t make: flat stock, pockets and profiles. Compressed air is on tap beside it.

    • Tormach xsTECH Pro
    • Fully enclosed, with a tool setter
    • Compressed air at the machine
    Safety sheet and instructions

  9. 09of 10

    Drill press and belt sander

    The Bosch PBD 40 bench drill shows its speed and drilling depth on a display. Next to it are a Proxxon TB 50 belt sander for edges, ends and small corrections, and a vacuum under the bench for the dust.

    • Bosch PBD 40 bench drill
    • Proxxon TB 50 belt sander
    • Workshop vacuum
    Safety sheet and instructions

  10. 10of 10

    The office

    The glass room between the two build tables is where you’ll find Nitai Bieber, the engineer who runs the makerspace. Not sure which machine fits the job? Knock.
